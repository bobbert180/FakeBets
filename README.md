# FakeBets

FakeBets is an app designed to help you track your betting activity without needing to use any money. I had a hunch I was a bad gambler, so I decided to do my first round of testing and after looking at the numbers, I was correct 47% of the time through the year. This solidified that I had no idea what I was doing and should try to learn more before throwing away money.

## Overview

With FakeBets, you can simulate your betting strategies without risking real money. By logging your hypothetical bets and outcomes, you learn just how little you know. This allows you to objectively analyze whether your approach would have been profitable or if the odds are truly stacked against you.

## Key Features

- **Track Bets:** Log each bet with details such as date, event, odds, stake, and outcome.
- **Simulate Strategies:** Test different betting strategies and see how they perform over time.
- **Analyze Results:** Visualize your performance with charts and statistics, including win/loss ratios, ROI, and streaks.
- **No Real Money Required:** All bets are simulated, so you can experiment risk-free.

## Why Use FakeBets?

- **Risk-Free Learning:** Perfect for beginners who want to learn about betting without financial risk.
- **Strategy Testing:** Experienced bettors can refine their strategies before committing real money.
- **Data-Driven Decisions:** Make informed choices based on historical data and trends.
